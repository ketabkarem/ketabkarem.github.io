# Kitab Karim - Privacy Policy & Terms

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Kitab Karim is a religious page that publishes Quran recitations, adhkar and hadith on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

The "kitabkarim publisher" app is an internal tool used only by the page owner to publish Kitab Karim's own content to Kitab Karim's official accounts, through the platforms' official APIs (Meta Graph API, YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API).

The app does not collect, read, store, sell or share any user data.

The permissions it requests are limited to publishing content to Kitab Karim's own accounts and reading the basic account information needed to publish.

Access tokens are stored only on the page owner's computer and are never uploaded to any other service.

No data is used for advertising or analytics.

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your platform account settings.

## Terms of Service

The app is for Kitab Karim's internal use only and is not offered to the public.

Published content comes from official, documented sources and is attributed to them.

Use of the app complies with each platform's terms and policies.
